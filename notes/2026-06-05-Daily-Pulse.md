> China has begun deploying underwater data centres as a way to reduce the enormous cooling demands of modern artificial intelligence infrastructure.

One such facility is located off the coast near Shanghai, positioned roughly 35 metres below the surface of the ocean. It reportedly houses close to 2,000 servers, including high performance GPU systems designed for AI processing, 5G applications, and other large scale computing tasks.

The concept is based on using the surrounding seawater as a natural cooling system. Conventional data centres require large amounts of electricity to prevent servers from overheating, especially when running AI workloads that generate significant heat. Cooling can account for a major share of total energy use.

By placing sealed server modules underwater inside pressure resistant units, operators take advantage of the consistently low temperatures found at depth. This reduces the need for traditional air conditioning and mechanical cooling systems.

Reports indicate the site has achieved a Power Usage Effectiveness score of under 1.15, which suggests that a greater proportion of its electricity is being used for computing rather than supporting infrastructure. For comparison, many standard data centres operate at around 1.5 or higher.

The project is said to have been developed by HiCloud Technology alongside Chinese partners, with an estimated cost of around 226 million dollars and a capacity of approximately 24 megawatts.

This approach reflects a wider trend in the technology sector as demand for AI computing power continues to rise rapidly. Engineers and companies are increasingly exploring alternative designs such as offshore facilities, floating platforms, and experimental cooling methods in response to growing energy requirements.

Underwater data centres remain an emerging solution, but they highlight the scale of innovation being explored to manage the environmental and energy pressures of modern computing.

> 🇨🇳 译文：中国已开始部署水下数据中心，作为减少现代人工智能基础设施巨大冷却需求的一种方式。

其中一处设施位于上海附近的海岸，位于海面以下约 35 米处。据报道，它拥有近 2,000 台服务器，包括专为人工智能处理、5G 应用和其他大规模计算任务而设计的高性能 GPU 系统。

该概念基于使用周围的海水作为自然冷却系统。传统数据中心需要大量电力来防止服务器过热，尤其是在运行产生大量热量的人工智能工作负载时。制冷占总能源消耗的主要部分。

通过将密封的服务器模块放置在水下耐压单元内，操作员可以利用深度持续的低温。这减少了对传统空调和机械冷却系统的需求。

报告显示，该站点的电力使用效率得分低于 1.15，这表明其大部分电力用于计算而不是支持基础设施。相比之下，许多标准数据中心的运行速度约为 1.5 或更高。

据称，该项目由HiCloud Technology与中国合作伙伴共同开发，预计成本约为2.26亿美元，装机容量约为24兆瓦。

随着对人工智能计算能力的需求持续快速增长，这种做法反映了科技领域更广泛的趋势。工程师和公司越来越多地探索替代设计，例如海上设施、浮动平台和实验冷却方法，以满足不断增长的能源需求。

水下数据中心仍然是一种新兴的解决方案，但它们凸显了为管理现代计算的环境和能源压力而探索的创新规模。
