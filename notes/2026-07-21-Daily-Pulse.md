> Jeff Bezos reveals why sleeping 4 hours a day would make him a worse CEO, not a better one:

"On sleep I get 8 hours of sleep. I prioritize it unless I'm traveling in different time zones. Sometimes it's impossible but I am very focused on it. I think better, I have more energy, my mood is better"

"Think about it as a senior executive, what do you really get paid to do? You get paid to make a small number of high-quality decisions. Your job is not to make thousands of decisions every day"

"Let's say that I slept 6 hours a day. Well, let's go really crazy and say I slept 4 hours a day. Now I just got four so-called productive hours back. If I was going to have 12 hours of productive time during any waking day, now all of a sudden I have 16 productive hours. So I have 33% more time to make decisions. If I was going to make a hundred decisions, now I can make 133 decisions"

"Is that really worth it if the quality of those decisions might be lower because you're tired or grouchy or any number of things?"

"It's different if it's a startup company. When Amazon was a hundred people it's a different story. But Amazon's not a startup company and all of our senior executives operate the same way I do. They work in the future. They live in the future"

"None of the people who report to me should really be focused on the current quarter. Sometimes we'll have a good quarterly conference call and Wall Street will like our quarterly results and people will stop me and say congratulations on your quarter. I say thank you, but what I'm really thinking is that quarter was baked three years ago. Right now I'm working on a quarter that's going to reveal itself sometime in the future"

"Why do I need to make a hundred decisions today? If I make three good decisions a day that's enough. And they should just be as high quality as I can make them. Warren Buffett says he's good if he makes three good decisions a year"

> 🇨🇳 译文：杰夫·贝佐斯 (Jeff Bezos) 揭示了为什么每天睡 4 小时会让他成为一名更糟糕的 CEO，而不是更好的 CEO：

“在睡眠方面，我有 8 个小时的睡眠时间。除非我在不同的时区旅行，否则我会优先考虑它。有时这是不可能的，但我非常专注。我想得更好，我有更多的精力，我的心情更好”

“想想作为一名高级管理人员，你真正获得报酬是做什么的？你获得报酬是为了做出少量高质量的决策。你的工作不是每天做出数千个决策”

“假设我每天睡 6 小时。好吧，让我们变得非常疯狂，假设我每天睡 4 小时。现在我刚刚恢复了 4 个所谓的高效时间。如果我在任何一天醒来时都有 12 小时的高效时间，那么现在我突然有 16 小时的高效时间。所以我有 33% 的时间来做决定。如果我要做出 100 个决定，现在我可以做出 133 个决定”

“如果这些决定的质量可能会因为你疲倦、脾气暴躁或其他原因而降低，那么这真的值得吗？”

“如果是一家初创公司，情况就不同了。当亚马逊有一百个人的时候，情况就不同了。但亚马逊不是一家初创公司，我们所有的高管都和我一样运作。他们在未来工作。他们生活在未来。”

“向我报告的人都不应该真正关注当前季度。有时我们会召开一次很好的季度电话会议，华尔街会喜欢我们的季度业绩，人们会阻止我并对你的季度表示祝贺。我说谢谢，但我真正想的是这个季度是三年前出炉的。现在我正在研究一个将在未来某个时候揭晓的季度。”

“为什么我今天需要做出一百个决定？如果我每天做出三个好的决定就足够了。而且它们应该是我能做出的高质量决定。沃伦·巴菲特说，如果他每年做出三个好的决定，他就很好”
