Here’s some more information

Section 24220 of the IIJA liked to the RIDE Act , Halt Drunk Driving provisions directs the Secretary of Transportation to issue a Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard requiring passenger motor vehicles to be equipped with “advanced drunk and impaired driving prevention technology.”

Keep in mind, this is NOT limited to drunk driving. It covers any type of impairment and is left intentionally vague, drowsiness is technically an “impairment”

The statute defines this as a system that can:

- Passively monitor driver performance to accurately identify impairment, and/or
- Passively and accurately detect whether the driver’s blood alcohol concentration is at or above the legal limit,
and then prevent or limit vehicle operation if impairment is detected

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration has issued reports saying this technology is not ready so it may be delayed again but the deadline is fast approaching

这是更多信息

IIJA 第 24220 条类似于《RIDE 法案》，“停止醉酒驾驶”条款指示交通部长发布联邦机动车辆安全标准，要求客运机动车辆配备“先进的醉酒和酒后驾驶预防技术”。

请记住，这不仅限于醉酒驾驶。它涵盖了任何类型的损伤，并故意含糊其辞，从技术上讲，嗜睡是一种“损伤”

该法规将其定义为一个可以：

- 被动监控驾驶员表现以准确识别损伤，和/或
- 被动准确检测驾驶员血液酒精浓度是否达到或高于法定限度，
